Pomodoro Clock privacy policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Pomodoro Clock does not collect or send extension data to a server. You do not need an account.

What stays on your device

Your timer state, settings, focus-session history and feedback prompt preferences stay in your local Chrome profile. They are not synced or shared. Removing the extension deletes its local data.

Network requests and external pages

The extension does not make network requests to run the timer. It works offline. Chrome may open these separate pages:

  • A welcome page when you install Pomodoro Clock.
  • An optional feedback page when you uninstall it.
  • A feedback page if you select 1–3 stars in the extension, or the Chrome Web Store reviews page if you select 4–5 stars.

The extension does not send your star rating, timer data or history to these pages. The welcome, uninstall and feedback pages use Umami to count visits. If you choose to send feedback, the text you enter and any reason you select go to Google Forms. Nothing is submitted automatically.

Chrome permissions

  • alarms lets the timer finish a session and update the toolbar countdown while the popup is closed.
  • storage saves the timer, settings, session history and feedback prompt preferences in your Chrome profile.
  • offscreen plays the bundled alarm sound without keeping the popup open.
  • notifications shows a local notification when a focus session or break ends. It is not used to send data.

Pomodoro Clock does not request access to any websites, read your browsing history or read information about your tabs.
